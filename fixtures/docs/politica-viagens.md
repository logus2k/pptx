# Travel policy

All business travel follows this policy. It applies to employees and contractors alike.

# Approval

Domestic trips are approved by the team manager. Any trip abroad must be approved in advance by a director, at least ten working days before departure.

# Flights

Economy class for flights under six hours. For longer flights, business class may be booked with the director's approval.

# Hotels

Hotels of up to four stars, close to the place of work. Suites and upgrades are not reimbursed.

# Meals

Lunch up to 25 euros and dinner up to 45 euros per day, alcohol excluded.

# Ground transport

Prefer public transport and taxis. Car rental only when there is no reasonable public transport, with the manager's approval.

# Expenses

Receipts are submitted in the expenses tool within 30 days of the end of the trip; later claims are not paid.
